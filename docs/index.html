.uk domains, Nominet and WHOIS

Nominet runs the .uk domain registry. WHOIS shows registration data such as registrar, creation date and name servers. Since GDPR most personal details are hidden, but creation date, registrar and name servers are still very useful.

Think of it like this: WHOIS is the land registry for domains: you may not see the owner's name, but you see when the plot was bought and who sold it.
Why it matters for UK clients

A lookalike .co.uk domain registered yesterday that imitates a client's brand is a strong phishing signal. New domain + brand name = high priority.

Enrichment tip

Add 'domain age in days' to every domain indicator. Domains under 30 days old deserve extra suspicion.

Try it today

Run a WHOIS lookup on any .co.uk domain and find its creation date and registrar.

IPs, ASNs and RIPE

Every public IP belongs to a network called an Autonomous System, identified by an ASN (for example AS2856 is BT). RIPE NCC is the registry for Europe, including the UK. RIPEstat (stat.ripe.net) shows who owns an IP, its ASN, country and routing history for free.

Quick quiz: Which regional registry covers UK IP addresses?
Show answerRIPE NCC.

The Admiralty Code: grading sources

The Admiralty (NATO) system grades intel with a letter and a number. Letter = source reliability (A completely reliable to E unreliable, F cannot be judged). Number = information credibility (1 confirmed by other sources to 5 improbable, 6 cannot be judged). B2 means usually reliable source, probably true.

Quick quiz: In the grade 'B2', what does the B describe and what does the 2 describe?
Show answerB is source reliability (usually reliable). 2 is information credibility (probably true).
