Companies House as an enrichment source

Companies House is the UK register of companies. Its free API returns a company's registered address, status, SIC codes (what industry it is in), officers (directors), filing history and parent companies.

Think of it like this: A UK company's birth certificate, CV and family tree in one place.
Why it matters for UK clients

When a UK victim appears on a ransomware leak site, Companies House tells you its sector, size and group structure. That lets you warn clients in the same sector or supply chain.

Enrichment tip

Map SIC codes to your sectors. For example 86xxx = human health, 64xxx = financial services, 69xxx = legal and accounting. Tag victims and clients the same way so matches are automatic.

Try it today

Search a UK company from today's ransomware list at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk and note its SIC code.

The PHIA probability yardstick

The UK Professional Head of Intelligence Assessment (PHIA) yardstick links words to probability ranges so 'likely' means the same thing to everyone. Roughly: remote chance (under 5%), highly unlikely (10-20%), unlikely (25-35%), realistic possibility (40-50%), likely (55-75%), highly likely (80-90%), almost certain (95% and above).

Quick quiz: Which yardstick term covers roughly 55-75%?
Show answerLikely (or probable).

The NCSC and what it gives you for free

The National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) is the UK's technical authority for cyber security and part of GCHQ. It publishes advisories, joint alerts with Five Eyes partners, guidance, and runs free services for UK organisations.
