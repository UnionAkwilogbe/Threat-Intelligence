IPs, ASNs and RIPE

Every public IP belongs to a network called an Autonomous System, identified by an ASN (for example AS2856 is BT). RIPE NCC is the registry for Europe, including the UK. RIPEstat (stat.ripe.net) shows who owns an IP, its ASN, country and routing history for free.

Think of it like this: The IP is a house number. The ASN is the estate or landlord who owns the whole street.
Why it matters for UK clients

Knowing whether a malicious IP sits in a UK ASN changes the response. UK hosting means UK law enforcement and UK providers can act, and it may mean a compromised UK business rather than attacker-owned infrastructure.

Enrichment tip

Always add ASN name and country to an IP. Flag known residential ISPs (BT, Sky, Virgin Media) because these IPs are often compromised home devices or proxies, not attacker servers.
